# 029 — LOO BudgetMemory Generalization with Repo-Key Fix (5×2 Fixed)

## 1. Purpose

Re-run of 027 with the repo-key normalization fix from 028 (`normalize_repo_key()`). Verifies that the BudgetMemory 4-tier cascade now correctly resolves to `repo_median` for sympy tasks instead of falling through to `strategy_median`.

## 2. Changes from 027

| item | 027 | 029 |
|------|-----|-----|
| repo-key normalization | broken — `sympy__sympy` vs `sympy` mismatch | fixed via `normalize_repo_key()` |
| sympy budget source | strategy_median (100%) | repo_median (100%) |
| strategy names | budget_tight_smart, budgetflow_tight | budget_only_tight, budgetflow_full_tight |
| NFS lock dir | `repo_cache/.locks/` (NFS, deadlocks) | `/tmp/budgetflow_locks/` (local) |

### 4.3 BudgetMemory Source Distribution

| source | count | pct |

**Zero exact_task leakage.** `--budget-memory-exclude-ids` correctly prevents held-out task matching.

**Repo-key normalization fix confirmed.** All 8 sympy rows resolve to `repo_median` (vs 0 in 027). Django rows (2) correctly fall through to `global_fallback` since training data has 0 django records after exclusion.

All 10 rows: `budget_memory_applied=True`, `budget_memory_hard_budget_used=False`.

### 4.4 Cascade Details

|cascade path|records |median| confidence|
|------------|--------|------|-----------|
| global_fallback | 70 | $0.1484 | low |
| repo_median (sympy)| 70 | $0.1484 | medium |

For django tasks, BM falls through: exact_task (0 records after exclude) → repo_median (0 django records) → strategy_median (not enabled) → global_fallback (70 records, median=$0.1484, risk_multiplier=2.0 → cap=$0.2968).

For sympy tasks, BM resolves at repo_median: 70 sympy records, median=$0.1484, risk_multiplier=1.43 → cap=$0.212.

Compared to 027's strategy_median cap of $0.1514 for sympy, the repo_median cap of $0.212 is more conservative (higher), which is appropriate for zero-shot generalization.

## 6. bo_tight vs bf_tight Comparison

bf_tight leads 4/5 vs 3/5 at 5×2, reversing 027's tie. The gap comes from django-10924: bo_tight's BudgetMemory estimated $0.2968 (global_fallback with 2× risk multiplier), and bo_tight exhausted it; bf_tight got the same cap but its adaptive pressure model spent more efficiently ($0.2804 with a pass).

At n=5 with 1 repeat, the 1-pass advantage is not statistically significant but is directionally consistent with 023/024 results at n=10.

## 7. Key Takeaways

1. **Repo-key normalization fix works.** 8/8 sympy rows hit repo_median (vs 0/8 in 027).
2. **LOO cascade correctness:** exact_task=0, repo_median=8, global_fallback=2. All tiers resolve correctly after exclude.
3. **NFS lock fix resolved.** Moving locks to `/tmp/budgetflow_locks/` eliminated the `nlmclnt_block` deadlock that killed two prior resume attempts.
4. **bf_tight leads 4/5 vs 3/5.** Single-pass margin, consistent with prior larger-scale results.
